LIMITATIONS
- Track is straight (the original image curves); train-local frame kept for reliable collision.
- Blaster is viewed more from its flank than the original's behind-and-above angle.
- Hands are posed meshes (no per-finger animation); pose changes are hidden by motion or a short squash.
- Fixed during the hand pass: blaster sockets (incl. muzzle) were measured in rig space and offset; now gun-local.
- Robot meshes are single pieces animated by vertex shear (no skeleton); defeat debris is simple toy parts.
- Playwright bot reads state through a dev-only hook to choose targets; all actions it takes are real input events.
- Recording was captured headless at 1280x720 (screencast frame rate is lower than the in-game 60 Hz sim).
