WHAT WORKS
- Title screen with level cards (stars/best moves from save), audio unlock on the first click.
- Three authored puzzles from data/world.json (copied to app/src/data/world.json): CONNECT, COMBINE,
  OPTIMIZE. Initial (broken) layouts are what players see; solved layouts are dev fixtures only.
- Build mode: BELT/TURN/SPLIT/MERGE placement, ERASE, R / Shift+R rotation, F mirror, hover ghost with
  in/out port arrows, invalid-edit reasons (fixed machine, outside board, nothing to erase, no moves),
  move budget, undo/redo, MIX/MOLD inspect fixed machines and explain recipes.
- Run mode: deterministic 1/60 s simulation, one candy per transport cell, reservations, backpressure,
  bounded machine buffers, splitter alternation, merge round-robin, mixer/star press/cooler/cube press
  recipes, wrong-ingredient blocking with feedback, jam badges + tooltip, pause, STOP & EDIT,
  per-ID single delivery, goal cards/progress bars from state, completion with stars, versioned save.
- Jelly mechanics (visual layer driven by sim state): squash/stretch springs, inertial lean on turns,
  stop squish, queued jiggle, pop on spawn/transform, hopper drop bounce, mixer funnel hop + swirl,
  cooler interior, press squash under the ram, arcs into bins with landing squash, completion confetti,
  goal-card bumps, post-jam repair hints (ROTATE TO CONNECT / PLACE A BELT TO CONNECT).
- Visuals: procedural candy-factory kit + fal-generated room backdrop and HUD icons; machine animation
  state-driven (paddle, ram, frost, star glow); quality selector (High: transmission gummies, 1.5 DPR cap;
  Low: emissive gummies, 1.0 DPR, smaller shadow map).
- Audio: fal-generated SFX and music, Web Audio, one pooled belt loop, mute + music/effects volume.
